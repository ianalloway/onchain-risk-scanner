## Gitcoin Description Blurb

On-chain Risk Scanner is a read-only contract triage tool for Ethereum and L2 builders. It helps researchers identify proxy metadata, privileged selectors, and opcode-level risk signals before deeper manual review. Funding supports verified-source ingestion, public scan sets, upgrade/admin timelines, and beginner-friendly white-hat research notes.
