# Starknet Seed Grants Fit Check

Application form: https://airtable.com/appfoRv2ottjRfTpL/pag0G55zA8aU4V9bD/form

Current fit: **not ready yet**.

Reason: Starknet Seed Grants support projects building in the Starknet ecosystem with an MVP or proof of concept. On-chain Risk Scanner currently supports EVM chains only and does not yet scan Starknet/Cairo contracts.

## How To Become Eligible

Add a Starknet module that:

- fetches contract class/hash data from Starknet RPC,
- inspects declared class metadata where available,
- maps proxy/account patterns used in Cairo contracts,
- outputs Starknet-specific reports,
- includes at least one public Starknet example report.

## Proposed Starknet Pitch

Starknet Risk Scanner would extend On-chain Risk Scanner to Cairo/Starknet contracts and produce read-only risk reports for account contracts, upgrade patterns, and protocol integrations. The project would support early-stage Starknet builders with security education and reproducible public reports.

## Suggested Ask

USD 10,000-15,000 equivalent in STRK after the Starknet proof of concept exists.
